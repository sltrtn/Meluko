# Meluko Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Meluko is a social alarm clock. This policy explains what information the Meluko Android app processes and why.

## Information Meluko processes

When you sign in with Google, Meluko receives and stores the account identifier needed to authenticate you, plus the name, email address, and profile photo that you choose to make available through Google Sign-In. You may also provide a username, bio, and profile image.

To provide the app's core features, Meluko stores your tribe membership, invite codes, shared alarm settings, proposed alarm times and vetoes, wake status, wake times, streaks, ranks, and total wakes. Tribe members can see the profile and wake-status information that is presented within their shared tribe.

Meluko stores selected alarm-tone preferences, alarm and tribe identifiers, and notice-dismissal preferences locally on your device. If you select a profile image, it is stored in Firebase Storage. Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, and Firebase Crashlytics process data on Meluko's behalf to provide authentication, synchronization, storage, and crash diagnostics.

## How Meluko uses information

We use this information to authenticate you, synchronize tribes and alarms, show shared wake progress, maintain streaks, provide support, and diagnose crashes. Meluko does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## Sharing

Your tribe-related profile and wake information is shared with the members of that tribe as part of the social-alarm experience. Data is also processed by Google and Firebase as service providers. We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable law or to protect the security of the service and its users.

## Retention and deletion

Meluko retains account and tribe data while your account is active. You can delete your account from the Account screen in the app. The app attempts to remove your profile data, alarm data, and stored profile photo before deleting the Firebase Authentication account. Some limited records may remain in backups or security logs for the period required to operate, secure, or comply with legal obligations.

Leaving or being removed from a tribe stops that tribe from being shown to you. Historical data that belongs to the tribe may remain visible to the tribe where necessary to preserve its shared history.

## Security

Meluko uses Firebase Authentication and Firebase Security Rules to restrict access to account and tribe data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Meluko is not directed to children. Do not use Meluko if you are below the minimum age required to consent to online services in your country.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or applicable law changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. For privacy questions or deletion help, open an issue at [Meluko support](https://github.com/sltrtn/Meluko/issues).
